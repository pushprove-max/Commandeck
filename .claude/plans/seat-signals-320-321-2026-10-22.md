# seat-signals-320-321-2026-10-22 -- one completion signal per seat, no duplicate turn-end messages, background senders can send (issues A and B)

Tier: Plan + judge. LOCAL merge only. Closes both issues of this batch: issue A, a finished plan seat read as stuck; issue B, turn-end messages to main (the main-watch sender bug folded into issue B by the owner,
2026-10-22). Changed-file tests plus the test files of every script edited; no red proofs, no test-timeout raises, no
new dependency, no feature flag. Version 0.28.0.

One principle governs every evidence check below: when the evidence is unsure, send. A duplicate message is
acceptable; a lost finish or a suppressed silent-stop alert is not. Every check that suppresses an alert or closes a
pane therefore requires positive, fresh, correctly addressed evidence; anything less sends.

## Main answers

- The seat record stores the plan file's blob at launch (`planref=`); a plan commit counts only when the committed plan differs from it.
- Completion evidence binds to the current episode (main-watch's episode start for the pane, or the seat's latest wait start), not to the seat's launch.
- A completion message counts only when addressed to the run, its coordinator or main's handle, and only on a marker line or a subject match, never a substring.
- A question or refusal ends on a line starting `QUESTION:` or `BLOCKED:`, and the Stop hooks send exactly one message for it; any other markerless ending sends nothing and the watcher's alert shows the pane's last words.
- One shared sender resolver, `pp_sender`, gives main-watch, `to-main.sh`, `cos-tell.sh` and the dashboard service's sends an explicit `--from`.
- Commit evidence goes through the finish/report path (main is told once); it never only cancels the stall alert.
- The watcher and collect claim a finish from the same completion evidence, so one schedule gives one notification.
- The hidden-file test asserts an empty `hidden.jsonl` and keeps the heartbeat-absence check.

## Why (measured 2026-10-22)

- issue A: a plan author that ends on a completion message ("Plan revised: ...", "Plan written: ...", sent as
  `worker_done`) and no `PLAN-DONE` line reads as unfinished everywhere. `seat.sh wait` exits 1 with
  `STOP ... route=stalled` (`seat.sh` ~703-710, ~737), `seat.sh collect` finds no verdict and keeps the pane open
  (`seat.sh` ~558, exit 3), and main-watch posts "stopped without reporting" (`main-watch.sh` ~910 `kind=silent`,
  ~1004). None of the three reads the seat's message or its plan commit.
- issue B: the worker Stop hooks already send nothing on a markerless turn (`claude-worker-notify.sh` ~36,
  `codex-notify.sh` ~24). The waste is duplicates. Orca's inbox (`orchestration inbox --limit 2000`, machine-wide)
  holds 379 `codex turn complete: VERDICT: GO|NO-GO` echoes (mostly the Codex passes a Builder runs for itself and reads
  itself), 103 `claude turn complete` echoes, and per plan author both its own `worker_done` ("Plan written") and
  `seat plan-author <unit>: PLAN-DONE` from collect, up to 3 times per unit. The same marker gate also drops a
  markerless question or refusal ("Which branch should I use?", "I cannot continue: access was refused."): nothing
  sends it, and no `StopFailure` handler is registered.
- main-watch: `main-watch.log` holds 33,332 "Could not determine the sender terminal" lines (32,700 heads-up task
  creates, the rest merge/seat/wait items, 11 refused sends). Cause: `resolve_main` (`main-watch.sh` ~448) takes the
  sender only from a pane in the main checkout whose `agentIdentity == "claude"`. Orca lists all 13 panes in
  the main checkout with `agentIdentity: null`, and the run's coordinator
  (`term_b012...`) is a live Claude pane in another checkout. So `MAIN_H` is empty, `${MAIN_H:+--from ...}` adds
  nothing, and the service (no terminal of its own) is refused on every task-create and send.
- Other background senders: the dashboard service keeps no terminal handle in its job env (`dashboard.sh` ~1188
  `keep`) and sends through `to-main.sh` and `cos-tell.sh --note` (`dashboard_serve.py` ~165, ~234). Both send with
  no `--from` (`to-main.sh` ~36, `cos-tell.sh` ~18), so they fail the same way from the service.

## Decisions (ponytail ladder: rung reached; lazier option rejected)

1. **The seat record is the evidence's anchor (rung 2, two fields on the existing record).** `seat.sh` launch
   (~402, the record's `printf`) also writes `planref=<blob>`: `git -C "$WT" rev-parse -q --verify
   "HEAD:.claude/plans/$UNIT.md"`, empty when no plan is committed at launch. `seat.sh wait` appends
   `waited=<epoch>` to the record when it starts (~597, and the relaunch ~684), as the relaunch already appends
   `relaunched=1`; `rec` reads the last one. Rejected (lazier): the commit time alone (a previous seat's commit in
   the replacement's launch second passes `>= launched`; a rebase rewrites `%ct` with no new work).
2. **Four small helpers in `paths.sh` after `pp_waited_on` (~333), one comment line each (rung 6, about 30 lines).**
   - `pp_seat_record <handle>` prints the path of the `seat-*.txt` record whose `handle=` is `<handle>` and that has
     no `origin=` line (the records main-watch's reader posts, ~1296-1301); nothing when none.
   - `pp_seat_since <record>` prints the current episode's start: the largest of the record's `launched=`, its last
     `waited=`, and main-watch's `$PP_SCRATCH/main-watch/episode-seat-<handle>-<task>` (`main-watch.sh` ~877 writes it
     when the same pane works again). Evidence must be strictly later than it: a same-second item is not counted,
     and the alert fires (the safe side).
   - `pp_plan_committed <record>`: true only for a `plan-author*` role when the committed plan's blob
     (`HEAD:.claude/plans/<unit>.md`) differs from `planref=` and the last commit touching it has `%ct` greater than
     `pp_seat_since`.
   - `pp_seat_done <handle> [recipient handle...]` prints `message`, `plan-commit` or nothing (exit 1). `message`:
     Orca's inbox (one `--limit 500` read, 10 s timeout) holds a message from `<handle>`, created after
     `pp_seat_since`, whose `to_handle` is `run:<run>` (the project's run file) or one of the recipient handles
     (with none given, the run's coordinator from `pp_run_coord`), and which carries completion on a line of its
     own: a line of its subject or body that, trimmed as `pp_waited_on` trims, equals `PLAN-DONE`, `<UNIT>-DONE` or
     `VERDICT: GO|NO-GO|DONE`, or a subject that starts with `Plan written` or `Plan revised` (case-insensitive). A
     marker inside a sentence ("Cannot print PLAN-DONE; permission denied.") is not one, and a message to another
     pane (`to_handle: term_helper`) is not one. Else `plan-commit` when `pp_plan_committed` is true. An inbox or
     run-list read that fails counts as no message: the alert still fires.
   - `pp_run_coord <run>` is `resolve_main`'s run-list read (~445), moved here so `pp_seat_done` and `pp_sender`
     (Decision 10) share it; `resolve_main` calls it, keeping its `?` on a failed read.
   Each caller ORs `pp_seat_done` with the marker read it already has; the marker reads are not merged. Rejected
   (lazier): matching `worker_done` by type alone (a `worker_done` that reports a failure is no finish; the planner
   subjects "Plan written"/"Plan revised" are what it sent); one merged marker+message function (each caller's marker
   read differs, pane vs session log).
3. **Who reads it and what it means. Every reader that counts evidence also hands the claim check the same marker
   (`PLAN-DONE`), so the watcher, wait and collect agree on ownership.**
   - main-watch `seat()`, in the `done` branch (~910) and the blank/`none` branch (~956), before `kind=silent`:
     `elif SD=$(pp_seat_done "$h" "$MAIN_H" "$COORD"); then`: `message` is a new kind `reported`: the item is written
     `handled`, nothing is posted (the seat's own message reached the run or main). `plan-commit` is
     `kind=finished`, and `RV_OUT` gains a last line `PLAN-DONE`, the marker a releasing wait records, so
     `pp_waited_on "$h" "$NOW" "$RV_OUT" "watch:$item"` (~986) claims as it does for a printed marker.
   - main-watch `seat()`, the `working` branch (~888-893): once `STALL_S` is reached, `pp_seat_done` is asked first.
     An answer settles the kind as above (kind and rv files written, `mark_pane f` taken, `$ST/sw-$key` touched) and
     falls through to the finish handling after the `case`, so a `plan-commit` is posted once as a finish or claimed
     by a wait, and a `message` is `handled`. No answer posts the stall as today. A settled seat Orca still reports
     `working` (the stale snapshot) is not a new episode: the episode reset (~877) also requires `pane_grew` when
     `sw-$key` exists, and the `working` branch returns 0 while `kind` is set. The reset removes `sw-$key` with the
     other files.
   - `seat.sh wait`: plan-author seats only. Before each of its three stall exits (~703, ~707-710, ~737) it calls
     `pp_seat_done "$H"`; an answer releases the wait as the marker (`SEEN_MARK=$MARKER`, exit 0), so the EXIT trap
     writes `seen <at> PLAN-DONE` as for a printed marker. Judges and reviewers are left alone: a judge's own message
     must never stand in for a `VERDICT: GO`.
   - `seat.sh collect`: plan-author seats only. No verdict and an answer gives `V=${DONE_MARKER:-PLAN-DONE}`, so the
     pane closes, the task completes and `plan_lines` records the plan hash `builder-launch.sh` ~146 reads. Its claim
     check (Decision 8) reads that same `V`.
   Rejected: a new watcher or state file for "done" (the seat record and the item files already hold it).
4. **The Stop hook makes the marker hard to skip (rung 6, about 8 lines in `claude-worker-notify.sh`).** When the pane
   has a seat record (`pp_seat_record`) whose role is `plan-author*`, the turn's last text has no `PLAN-DONE` line,
   `stop_hook_active` is not true, and `pp_plan_committed` is true for that record, the hook prints
   `{"decision":"block","reason":"<line>"}` and exits 0, sending nothing. The exact line:
   `Your plan .claude/plans/<unit>.md is committed since this seat started, but your turn ended without its marker. Print this line alone as your last line, with nothing after it: PLAN-DONE`
   The agent's next turn ends on `PLAN-DONE`, which wait, collect and main-watch already read. `stop_hook_active`
   stops a second block (the `cos-inbox-waiter-stop.sh` pattern). `PLAN-DONE` is the one plan-author marker
   (`builder-launch.sh` ~146 accepts only `PLAN-DONE*`). Rejected (lazier): printing the marker to the hook's own
   stdout (a Stop hook's stdout never reaches the session log or the pane, so no reader sees it). Codex has no
   blocking Stop hook: a Codex plan author relies on Decision 3's `plan-commit` (stated limit).
5. **The prompts end on the marker and name the ask lines (rung 2, existing prompt strings).** `seat.sh`'s
   `SPEC_TEXT` (~385) gains, for every role: `A question or a refusal goes on a line of its own that starts QUESTION: or BLOCKED:.`;
   for a `plan-author*` role it also gains: `Your last line, alone, is PLAN-DONE: a message saying the plan is done does not replace it.`
   `dispatch-task.sh`'s prompt (~65) gains the same `QUESTION:`/`BLOCKED:` sentence, and
   `templates/_builder-brief-template.md` (~198, after `Print {UNIT}-DONE as your last line and stop.`) gains it for
   a Builder's question to main. Judges keep their VERDICT line unchanged.
6. **The worker Stop hooks are silent for a seat pane's marker turn (rung 2: the seat record main-watch already
   reads).** Both hooks call `pp_seat_record` in their existing `pp_waited_on` subshell (`claude-worker-notify.sh`
   ~55, `codex-notify.sh` ~43): a record means "waited", no send. A seat's finish is reported by its wait/collect or
   by main-watch; the echo was the second copy. Panes with no record (`dispatch-task.sh` workers, hand-opened panes)
   keep their echo as their one report. Decision 9's ask lines are not silenced here. Rejected: deleting the sends
   outright (unrecorded panes would lose their only report).
7. **A Builder's own Codex passes never echo (rung 2, the env flag that already exists).** `codex-notify.sh`, after
   the type check ~19: `[ "${COMMANDECK_BUILDER:-}" != 1 ] || exit 0`. `worktree-setup.sh` ~241 sets it only in a
   Builder worktree's `settings.local.json` env, so only processes the Builder's session spawns carry it, its
   `codex exec` passes among them; the Builder reads those verdicts itself (`builder-codex-pass.sh`).
   `claude-worker-notify.sh` takes no such check (the Builder's own pane is covered by Decision 6). This breaks the
   two hooks' parity on purpose; both headers say so.
8. **One report per finished seat at collect (rung 2, two conditions on the existing send, `seat.sh` ~553).** The
   send also requires (a) a `seat.sh wait` claimed the finish (`pp_waited_on "$H" "" "$V"$'\n'"$OUT"`, with `V` the
   evidence's marker of Decision 3): a finish main-watch posted, collected later by main, sends nothing, as main ran
   collect and reads its output; and (b) `pp_seat_done` did not print `message`: the seat's own report already
   reached main. Rejected (lazier): deleting collect's send (a `seat.sh run` in the background is waited, so
   main-watch stays silent for it, and collect's send is then its only report).
9. **Markerless actionable endings have one delivery path (rung 6, about 6 lines per hook, in parity).** Each hook
   reads `ASK`, the first line of the turn's last text that, trimmed of leading `*`, `` ` ``, `•`, `>` and spaces,
   starts with `QUESTION:` or `BLOCKED:`. With an `ASK` the hook skips the marker gate, the driver check
   (`hs_unit_driven`), the `pp_waited_on`/seat-record silence and the Builder flag, keeps only the per-turn
   `notified-<handle>` dedupe (keyed `<turn id> <ASK>`), and sends one `question` to `run:<run>` with subject
   `claude|codex turn complete: QUESTION|BLOCKED (<unit>)` and the turn's last text as body. A wait or driver that
   also reports the stop is an accepted duplicate. Any other markerless ending sends nothing: main-watch's
   "stopped without reporting" post (~1004) and `seat.sh wait`'s idle STOP line (~707-710) catch it, and both gain
   the pane's last words: the post's body ends `Last words: <last 300 characters of RV_OUT, newlines as spaces>`;
   the STOP line gains `last message: $(kv last_message)`, as its first-turn twin (~704) already has. Rejected
   (lazier): guessing a question from a trailing `?` or refusal wording (a guess both misses and over-sends);
   relying on `StopFailure` (only an API error fires it, and no handler is registered).
10. **One sender resolver for every background sender (rung 6, about 6 lines in `paths.sh`).** `pp_sender
    [handle...]` prints the first of: its non-empty arguments, `$ORCA_TERMINAL_HANDLE`, main's live pane
    (`pp_seat_pane main "$PP_MAIN"`), the run's coordinator (`pp_run_coord`) when `terminal list` shows it live;
    nothing (exit 1) when none. Users:
    - main-watch: `resolve_main` sets `FROM=$(pp_sender "$MAIN_H" "<COORD when COORD_LIVE is not 0 and COORD is not ?>")`;
      `--from` on the task-create (~217), the sends (~357, ~502) and the retry swap (~395) reads `FROM`. `run-use`
      (~411) keeps `MAIN_H`. `MAIN_H` keeps its meaning for typing a wake: nothing is typed into a pane outside the
      main checkout.
    - `to-main.sh` (~36) and `cos-tell.sh --note` (~18): `${FROM:+--from "$FROM"}` with `FROM=$(pp_sender)`.
    - The dashboard service sends only through those two scripts (`dashboard_serve.py` ~165, ~234; `decision.sh`
      sends nothing itself), so they cover it; its job env keeps no terminal handle, unchanged.
    A worker pane's `to-main.sh` keeps today's sender (its own handle), now passed explicitly. Rejected: dropping
    the `agentIdentity` filter (a shell pane in the main checkout would become main and be typed into); a fixed
    handle in config (main restarts under new handles); a second resolver per script.
11. **Dead filter goes (rung 1, delete), and ask echoes are never hidden.** `orca-wait.sh` `noisy` ~260-262 loses its
    markerless half (no hook sends a markerless echo except Decision 9's ask lines, which main must see); its
    `driven(echo_unit)` half stays but excludes a subject matching `turn complete: (QUESTION|BLOCKED)`. Rule 1's
    comment ~220-221 becomes `1. Dropped (issue B): ...`, and ~532-535 loses the markerless sentence. The
    quiet-wakes unit's wake filter (`pp_inbox_actionable`, `wakeKinds`, held notes) is untouched.
12. **Idle/stuck watchers keep their own timers (rung 1).** They run on their own clocks and read no hook message:
    main-watch's tick with `STALL_S`/`WAITING_S` (`main-watch.sh` ~50-51; stall post ~893, waiting post ~977,
    stopped post ~1004); `seat.sh wait`'s idle limit (`kv stalled`, ~628, ~707-710); `close-done-panes.sh`
    `QUIET_SECONDS`/`ORPHAN_SECONDS` (~36-37). Only Decision 3's evidence check and Decision 9's last words change
    them. Liveness stays local: every hook fire writes its row through `hook_fire_log_arm`
    (`claude-worker-notify.sh` ~11, `codex-notify.sh` ~13) before any exit.

## Stated limits

- A pane with no seat record that sends `worker_done` and also ends on a marker still gives two messages (turn start
  is not known to the hook without a transcript walk). Every plugin seat has a record.
- A Codex plan author has no blocking Stop hook; its unmarked committed plan is caught by main-watch and wait
  (`plan-commit`), not by an asked-for marker.
- Evidence in the same second as its episode start, a report older than the newest 500 machine-wide messages, or a
  report sent before a later `seat.sh wait` started is not counted: the alert fires (safe side, a duplicate at most).
- A question or refusal without its `QUESTION:`/`BLOCKED:` line sends nothing; the stopped/idle alert carries its last
  words instead.
- With no terminal handle, no main pane and no live coordinator, `pp_sender` finds no sender; that is a real outage,
  logged as today.
